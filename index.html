How this is calculated +

Activity is corrected to the time of administration using the selected radionuclide’s physical half-life.

Ainjection = Ameasured × 2−Δt / T½

When residue is included, its later reading is corrected backward to the injection time, then subtracted:

Rinjection = Rmeasured × 2+Δtresidue / T½
Anet = Ainjection − Rinjection

Δt is elapsed time; T½ is physical half-life. Use the same radionuclide for both readings. Residue must be measured at or after injection. This assumes an injection at the entered time and does not model a prolonged infusion.

1 mCi = 37 MBq. This calculates activity, not absorbed dose. It does not account for biological clearance, measurement uncertainty, or calibration errors.

Dates use your device’s time zone. Enter the actual dates for overnight calculations. Ambiguous or nonexistent daylight-saving times cannot be used.

Before clinical use, validate the calculator, half-lives, and rounding against your department’s approved procedure.
